# Close-focus GUI derivatives

Derived from the user-supplied ACM Extended GUI art and the supplied Advanced Combat Medicine and ACE3 medical-interface artwork. Changes: neutral luminance conversion, premultiplied-alpha Gaussian defocus, DXT5 mipmaps and lossless LZO compression of the encoded blocks. Original assets remain untouched.

The per-texture source paths and hashes are in tools/nv_texture_manifest.json. ACE3 project copyright and license are preserved in ACE_ASSET_LICENSE.txt. The supplied ACM archive contains no project-wide artwork license; its nested audio-credit file does not license these GUI images. No claim of new ownership or blanket relicensing is made. Retain the original authors' attribution and verify distribution permissions for your release.

Runtime NVG borders are read from the actual equipped item's ACE configuration or active ACE title. Those mask textures are referenced at runtime and are not replaced or redistributed here.
